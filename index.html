Urban Living in
Edgewater

Second Plaza Tower is a luxury apartment community at 222 NE 25th Street in Miami’s Edgewater neighborhood — the second phase of Biscayne Park. Twelve stories hold 90 residences and 10,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, with 166 covered parking spaces.

Homes range from a 750-square-foot one bedroom to a 1,213-square-foot three bedroom, each with a private terrace. Residences are finished with granite countertops, wood floors in living rooms, and a full-size stacked washer and dryer. A swimming pool, fitness center, and on-site office complete the lifestyle.

Features & Amenities

Everything you need for the Miami lifestyle — at home, poolside, and on 25th Street.

Swimming Pool

Lounge poolside under the palms with the tower as your backdrop. A sun deck, lounge chairs, and a spa-side wading area make it easy to unwind after a day in the city.

Fitness Center

Stay on your routine without leaving the building. The fitness center has the cardio equipment you need for a complete workout at home.

Covered Parking

Come home to 166 covered parking spaces. Assigned garage parking keeps your car out of the Miami sun — steps from the elevator.

Retail at Your Door

Ten thousand square feet of ground-floor retail puts everyday conveniences at the base of the tower — part of Edgewater’s walkable 25th Street corridor.
